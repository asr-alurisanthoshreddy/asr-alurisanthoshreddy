## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🗺️ Tourism Management System — Salesforce Platform

> *Sep 2025 · Salesforce · Apex · Flows · REST APIs · Dashboards*

Travel agencies ran bookings entirely on spreadsheets and email — no enforcement, no cross-team visibility, no automation. I replaced the entire workflow.

**What I built:**
- 🔒 Seat reservation locked at transaction time via Apex → **zero overbooking**
- 📋 Travel itineraries auto-generated on booking confirmation with Flows
- ⏰ Payment reminder triggers scheduled with Batch & Scheduled Apex
- 🔗 Hotel and transport partners connected through Named Credential REST APIs
- 📊 Real-time regional dashboards for revenue and occupancy (replaced ad-hoc manual reports)

**Impact:** `~60% cut in manual workload` · `100% elimination of double-bookings` · `Live visibility across all regions`

---

### 🌸 HSSAN — Hybrid Spectral-Spatial Attention Network

> *Nov 2025 · PyTorch · Deep Learning · Computer Vision · Hyperspectral Imaging*

Standard CNNs stalled at ~84% on fine-grained flower recognition because RGB alone can't separate visually similar species. I designed a solution.

**What I built:**
- 🧩 Multi-modal input fusion: **RGB + Multispectral + Hyperspectral** images
- 🔷 2D and 3D convolution layers for spectral-spatial feature extraction
- 🎯 Dual attention mechanism (channel + spatial) — spectral distinctions invisible to single-modality
- 📐 Ablation study validating every architectural component independently

**Results:**

| Model | Accuracy | F1 Score |
|-------|----------|----------|
| RGB-only CNN baseline | ~84% | — |
| **HSSAN (ours)** | **92.8%** | **91.8%** |
| Improvement | **+6 to +8 pts** | ✅ |

---

### 🩺 Cervical Cancer Risk Prediction

> *Jun 2025 · Python · XGBoost · EDA · Feature Engineering · Medical AI*

Manual cervical cancer screening is slow, clinician-dependent, and often late. I built an ML classifier that gives teams explainable, actionable signals.

**What I built:**
- 🔬 XGBoost binary risk classifier on patient clinical data
- 🧹 End-to-end preprocessing, EDA, and feature engineering pipeline
- 📊 Feature importance analysis — identified top clinical risk predictors
- 🧾 Interpretable outputs (not black-box scores) for medical use

**Result:** `95.35% accuracy` · `Explainable top-risk-factor ranking` · `Actionable signals for clinician follow-up priority`
